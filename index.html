Parcel 3D Localization & Grasping Estimation
Nov - Dec 2025AI / CV Developer

Computer Vision system processing RGB-D images to precisely determine 3D coordinates (x, y, z) and rotation angles (Rx, Ry, Rz) to guide Robotic Arms for parcel grasping.

  • Semantic Segmentation: Automated data prep and YOLOv11 (yolo11n-seg) training to extract parcel masks from RGB data.
  • 2D-to-3D Projection: Backprojected 2D pixels into 3D space using Depth Maps and Camera Intrinsics.

  • Optimization: Randomly sampled 500 valid depth points to massively speed up SVD computation without losing accuracy.
